1. Problem Definition

Anatomy of a $60B Information Asymmetry

High stakes, conflicting figures, and opaque referral black boxes

01. Conflicting Truths

Different reports quote wildly divergent counts (ranging from 1,600 to 2,200 GCCs). No single public registry tracks which companies operate where, which specific functions they house, or which tier-2 cities are truly scaling versus marketing hype.

02. Misaligned Incentives

Companies deal with an opaque chain: management consultants, local lawyers, tenant brokers, and recruiters. Brokers push high-commission leases; recruiters benefit from high employee churn and wage inflation.

03. Costly Trial & Error

New entrants spend $1.5M–$3M on retainers and roadshows, yet end up relying on informal WhatsApp networks, outdated annual PDFs, and golf-course referrals to make irreversible 10-year commitments.

What they do today: Pay $200k+ for static Big-4 slide decks, hire local brokers who only show properties they represent, or spend 6 months conducting subjective executive tours.

5. Solution & Architecture

GCC Atlas: Deterministic Math + Grounded AI

A production-grade dual-engine platform connected live to Supabase & OpenRouter

1. Plan Wizard (Inputs)

Collects target functions (Tech, Finance, Ops), 1-yr & 3-yr headcount, seniority ratio, and user-assigned factor weights (1–5) across talent, rent, and costs.

2. Deterministic Core

Calculates Min-Max normalized scores across 45 candidate cities. Missing data is never guessed; scores are 100% reproducible and immune to LLM hallucination.

3. Side-by-Side Compare

Compares 2 to 5 locations across identical metrics: GCCPROs verified center counts, Randstad salary tiers, and JLL Grade A office rates.

4. Cost Scenario Engine

Bottom-up modeling combining verified annual FTE salaries and Grade A office leases with an explicit exclusions audit trail.

5. Ask Atlas AI (RAG)

Conversational assistant grounded in Supabase records via OpenRouter (Gemini Flash 1.5) with mandatory source citations and honest refusals.

6. Live Supabase Layer

16 Supabase tables with 45 cities, 112 location metrics, 77 salary rows, 71 candidate evidences, and NSWS statutory approval prompts.
